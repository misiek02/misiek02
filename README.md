### About

Control Engineering & Robotics graduate from **Wrocław University of Science and Technology** (Embedded Robotics). I build perception and localization systems for robots — from LiDAR scan matching in ROS 2 to deep-learning trackers for UAVs in infrared video. Outside of research I write native Android apps and tinker with embedded hardware.

- 🎓 **MSc thesis (2026):** multi-object tracking of airborne objects in infrared video
- 🤖 **BEng thesis (2024):** scan-matching methods for LiDAR-based robot localization
- 🛸 **Multi-robot systems:** consensus control for Crazyflie drone swarms
- 📱 **On the side:** Kotlin / Jetpack Compose apps, embedded C++, Arch + Hyprland

📄 Theses and project reports: [CV_resources](https://github.com/misiek02/CV_resources)
